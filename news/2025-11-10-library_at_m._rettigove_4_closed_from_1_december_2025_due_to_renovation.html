We are expanding and improving our spaces for studying and meeting.

Please make sure to borrow all the books you will need in time – the last day for borrowing is Friday, 28 November 2025.

During the closure:

  • all loans due during this period will be automatically extended until 4 May 2026,
  • books can be returned at any time via the return box next to the porter’s lodge,
  • borrowing (including through reservation boxes) will not be possible,
  • the study room in Celetná 13 will remain open as usual.

For the latest updates, please follow the faculty website and social media. Thank you for your understanding – we look forward to welcoming you back in our renewed library.
